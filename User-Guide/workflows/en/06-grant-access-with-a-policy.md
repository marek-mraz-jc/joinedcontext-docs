# Grant access with a policy

A policy grants a group, a role, a person or a service account the right to read or change data in one context space. The form says the grant back to you in words as you fill it. It is for data stewards and administrators.

## 1. Fill the form and read it in words

Open **Policies** and click **New Policy**. Give it a **Name**, pick the **Context Space**, choose the **Kind of grantee** and the **Grantee** from the names the Portal offers. Tick what they may do, for example **Read**. **Valid to** ends the grant on a date. **In words** says what the policy grants, so you can check it before anyone approves it.

![The policy form with a group, Read ticked, and the grant written as a sentence](../../img/en/policy-1-form-in-words.png)

## 2. Propose it

Click **Propose change**. The policy applies once a second person approves it.

![The policy proposed as a change](../../img/en/policy-2-proposed.png)
