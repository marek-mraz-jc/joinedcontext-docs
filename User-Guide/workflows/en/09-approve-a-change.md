# Approve a change

Every change to a project waits in **Approvals** until someone other than its author approves it. This page is for approvers.

## 1. Find the waiting change

Open **Approvals**. **Waiting for a decision** lists each proposed change with its author and **Risk lane**.

![A proposed change waiting in Approvals](../../img/en/approval-1-waiting.png)

## 2. Approve it

Open the change and read what it does. Click **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason the author reads. A red-lane change asks you to type the resource's name first. After approval, the page shows the change being applied.

![The change approved and being applied](../../img/en/approval-2-approved.png)

## 3. Find it in the history

**History** lists closed changes. **Name contains** with **Filter** finds one by the name of what it changed, with who approved it.

![The history filtered by name, showing the merged change and its approver](../../img/en/approval-3-history.png)
