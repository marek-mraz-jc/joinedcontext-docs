# Generate an app

An app is a small web application on one of the project's endpoints. You describe it in a sentence and the Portal builds it, with a preview on real data. It is for data stewards and anyone building a view for others.

## 1. Describe it and read the preview

Open **Applications** and click **Generate your own app**. Pick the **Endpoint**, say **What should the app do?**, give an **App name** and click **Generate the app**. The build reads samples through the endpoint, and the preview shows the app with that data.

![A generated app's preview showing rows read through the endpoint](../../img/en/app-1-generated-preview.png)

## 2. Open it in the Portal

Once published, **Open app** runs it inside the Portal with your sign-in. **Open in new window** gives it a window of its own.

![The app open inside the Portal](../../img/en/app-2-open-in-portal.png)
