# Open data sources of Barcelona (Open Data BCN)

Which datasets of the Open Data BCN catalogue (`opendata-ajuntament.barcelona.cat`) the `barcelona`
project takes, in which batch, as which Smart Data Model, and which it leaves out with the reason.
It follows [Open data integration rules](../Development/12-open-data-integration.md) and has the shape
of [Open data sources of Helsinki](helsinki-open-data.md). The owner order of 2026-10-22 asks for
every usable dataset in NGSI-LD, validated so it makes sense; task T-5261 built this page, tasks
T-5262 to T-5266 build the pipelines.

## 1. How the matrix was made

1. **Snapshot.** The supervisor's `package_search` snapshot of 2026-10-22 lists 555 datasets of 20
   theme organisations. A copy is in `Research/data/barcelona-opendata-snapshot-2026-10-22.tsv`.
   Three datasets appeared after it (section 6.3).
2. **Package read.** One `package_search?rows=1000` call on 2026-10-23 at 04:12Z returned all 558
   package records with their resources (the per-package `package_show` answered 503 to 227 of 555
   calls at 6 in parallel: the portal rate-limits). Every package is CC BY 4.0; the catalogue entry
   carries the attribution "Font: Ajuntament de Barcelona, Open Data BCN, CC BY 4.0".
3. **Probe.** One ranged GET (64 KiB, 20 s timeout, at most 3 in parallel, retry on 503) of the
   newest resource of each package. 547 answered 200. The `probe` column also holds two other
   answers, each checked by hand:
   - `302 token page`: four packages (`estat-estacions-bicing`, `informacio-estacions-bicing`,
     `aparcaments-sota-superficie`, `cataleg-circulars-bcnroc`) mark a resource `token_required`, and
     its URL answers 302 to `/tokens?resource_id=...`, the registration page. The platform creates no
     account: the batch blocks those datasets with `@user` until the owner registers.
   - `404 newest, 2023 200`: the eight income-atlas packages list a 2024 file added on 2026-10-22 at
     20:02Z whose URL answers 404; the 2023 file answers 200.
4. **Freshness read.** The package `metadata_modified` date says little: 88 of the 101 packages of the
   Equipaments family show 2023-10, yet their files are regenerated. `sanitat-farmacies` read in
   full on 2026-10-23 holds 1,020 records, the newest modified 2026-10-14; `agenda-cultural` holds
   events modified 2026-10-22. The `update` column is therefore the package's own frequency field.
5. **Judgement.** Each row was read by hand: the title, the formats, the frequency, the resource head
   from the probe (columns, district and neighbourhood codes) and, for the families below, a full
   file. 108 rows sit in another batch than the theme organisation suggests; the `note` column says
   "moved from <theme>" for those.

## 2. Decisions

- **integrate**: the dataset becomes NGSI-LD entities of one Smart Data Model (or a custom model
  that borrows the members of the named closest SDM class) through a pipeline of the named batch.
- **statistics**: a table of figures becomes `KeyPerformanceIndicator` series through the one
  statistics pipeline of T-5266, one indicator per table and dimension cut, the yearly files of one
  series merged into its history (`observedAt` is the period), linked to the district,
  neighbourhood or census section of T-5264.
- **skip**: nothing is built; the reason is in the row and in section 7.

Rules that decided many rows at once:

- **One pipeline per family.** 101 rows (`equipament-*`, `culturailleure-*`, `sanitat-*`,
  `serveissocials-*`, `np-*`, `allotjaments-*`, `educacio-*`, `esports-*` and other lists with the
  same CSV and JSON pair) are views of one register, the city's Equipaments database, in one JSON
  schema (`register_id`, `addresses[].location`, `classifications_data`). Proven on 2026-10-23: all
  1,020 `register_id` of `sanitat-farmacies` are among the 2,202 of `equipament-sanitat`. T-5265
  reads them in one pipeline and keeps one `PointOfInterest` per `register_id`, its categories the
  union of the lists it appears in. The cultural agenda (`agenda-cultural`, `agenda-diaria`,
  `agenda-activitats-esportives`) uses the same schema with `core_type` event; its venues are
  register entries, so each `Event` links its `PointOfInterest`.
- **Statistics in one batch.** Every table of figures goes to T-5266, whatever its theme: housing
  (63 rows of `habitatge`) moves there from T-5264, vehicle registers, flight counts and traffic
  fines from T-5262, the resilience-atlas grids from T-5263. Traffic fines
  (`denuncies_sancions_transit_bcn_detall`) carry no plate or person but one row per fine:
  T-5266 aggregates them per district, month and code.
- **Joined rows.** The five accident detail tables become attributes of the `RoadAccident` of
  `accidents-gu-bcn` (joined by file number, ages and sexes only as counts); zones, timetables and
  tariffs of surface parking become one `OnStreetParking`; the traffic state (`trams`,
  `itineraris`) refers to the sections of `transit-relacio-trams`.
- **Code lists** (`*-tipologia-*`, `*_codis`, `pad-dimensions`, the biodiversity species tables)
  are skipped as datasets: the mapping of the table they describe reads them for labels.
- **Documents, ledgers and persons.** The BCNROC repository indexes (titles and PDF links), photo
  catalogues, procurement and grant ledgers, personnel administration and the agendas and names of
  office holders hold no city object or indicator; they are skipped and stay links in the source
  catalogue. Budget and tax-roll totals are statistics with a minus point (section 6.1).
- **Ended series** whose current successor is in the matrix are skipped (the 2018-2019 Bicing
  archive, the old bike-lane ZIPs, the COVID-19 terraces).

## 4. Outputs

The `outputs` column names the representations the batch configures on the dataset's Endpoint,
following the [representation contract](../API/11-representation-contract.md):

- `ngsi-ld`: the broker view, always.
- `temporal`: the NGSI-LD history, only where values change over time.
- `geojson` and `ogc-features`: where the entities carry a geometry.
- `csv`: points and tables a spreadsheet user wants.
- `ical`: events with a start time.
- `feed`: an Atom feed, only where items appear over time (events, traffic notices, road works);
  off by default.

## 5. Checks every batch runs

The owner order of 2026-10-22 adds plausibility checks beside the SDM schema check. Each batch
task runs them on the real source data and writes the result into its own body:

- **Location**: every point or area inside the city limit of `limits-municipals-districtes` plus
  1 km; no 0,0; no swapped latitude and longitude. Many sources give ETRS89 UTM 31N (EPSG:25831)
  beside or instead of WGS84; the pipeline converts and the check runs on the converted point.
- **Values**: physical ranges per property (temperature -40 to 50 °C, percentages 0 to 100, counts
  at least 0, occupancy at most capacity, speeds 0 to 150 km/h) and the units the SDM names; dates no
  more than 1 day in the future and not older than the source claims.
- **Identity**: no two entities for one real object (same id, or same name within 20 m; the
  Equipaments family by `register_id`); every reference (`refDevice`, `refParkingSite`, area links,
  an event's venue) resolves to an existing entity.
- **Completeness**: the entity count matches the source record count (within 1 % for live feeds); a
  source that returns 0 records or data older than 3 update periods fails the run visibly and
  deletes nothing.
- **Human read**: 5 random entities per dataset compared with the source page or map, with the link.

Each pipeline fixture holds one out-of-area point, one out-of-range value and one duplicate, and the
pipeline drops or flags each with a counted reason. Archives (7z, ZIP) unpack with a size and
file-count cap and no path outside the work directory; fetches go only to
`opendata-ajuntament.barcelona.cat`, the one host every resource of the 558 packages names.

## 6. The matrix

### 6.1 Usefulness score and the 100 target

The owner order of 2026-10-22 asks the batches to build the useful rows first. Each integrate and
statistics row scores one point for each of:

- **live**: the package updates at least monthly (real time, 5 or 30 minutes, hourly, daily, weekly,
  monthly);
- **city question**: it answers a citizen or city question: traffic, parking, transit, bikes,
  events, services, air, noise, weather or water sensors, works, or a statistic broken down by
  district, neighbourhood or census section;
- **joins**: it joins entities the space already holds or another row adds (districts and
  neighbourhoods, sensors, sections, venues, register ids);

and loses one point when it is a budget table, a tax-roll total, a plan index or a one-off study
(the 2022 resilience atlas). A row scoring 1 or more is **useful**; a batch builds its useful rows
highest score first and leaves the rest listed. A skip row has no score.

| T-5262 | 35 | 2 |
| T-5263 | 23 | 1 |

The owner order of 2026-10-22 sets a floor of 100 live Smart Data Models datasets per city (counted
by T-5275). The 101 Equipaments rows share one register, so they count as datasets in the catalogue
but not as 101 sources. Without them Barcelona has 95 useful entity datasets and 172 useful
statistics series, so the floor is reached from useful rows alone and no gap remains. Two of the
highest-scoring sources (Bicing stations, B:SM car parks) wait for the owner's token (section 1).

### 6.5 T-5266 build state (2026-10-23)

One `statistics` pipeline reads every table below from Open Data BCN's CKAN DataStore: package_show
names the newest yearly CSV resource the DataStore holds, and one `datastore_search_sql` sums its
census sections to barri, district and city (GROUPING SETS) in the source's database. Each value
is one `KeyPerformanceIndicator` with `refArea` to the T-5264 AdministrativeArea; the newest period
is `kpiValue` and its history keeps one row per period. Three public datasets read them by theme:
