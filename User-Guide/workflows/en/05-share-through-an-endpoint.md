# Share data through an endpoint

An endpoint is the address through which other people and programs read a space. You choose the audience, the formats and the attributes it leaves out. It is for data stewards.

## 1. Fill the endpoint form

Open **Endpoints** and click **New endpoint**. Give it a **Name** and a **Title**, pick the **Context Space** and the **Audience**: **Selected projects**, **Organization**, or **Public** for anyone with the address. Tick the formats it serves. Under **Published attributes**, type an attribute and click **Hide** to leave it out.

![The endpoint form with a public audience, two formats and one hidden attribute](../../img/en/endpoint-1-form.png)

## 2. Propose the change

Click **Propose change**. The endpoint waits in **Approvals** until a second person approves it.

![The endpoint proposed as a change](../../img/en/endpoint-2-proposed.png)

## 3. See it live

Once approved, the endpoint appears as **Live** in the list after the next sync, usually within a few minutes, and its address answers.

![The endpoint listed as Live](../../img/en/endpoint-3-live.png)
